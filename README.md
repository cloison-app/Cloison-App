# privacy
Privacy Policy for Cloison App

1. Privacy Policy (for cloison.art/privacy)
Cloison Privacy Policy Last updated: November 7, 2026

Cloison ("the app") is a pin-collecting app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: feedback@cloison.art

What we collect:

Account: your email address and password (or your Apple ID relay email if you use Sign in with Apple), and the display name you choose.

Your collection: pin photos, pin details (names, makers, series, notes) and your board layout. These are stored in your account so they sync across your devices and can be restored.

Feed content: posts, comments, likes, reports and blocks you make. Posts, comments and your display name are visible to other signed-in users. If you share a pin to the Feed, your public profile and board may be visible to other signed-in users.

Feedback: messages you send from Settings, along with your account email so we can reply.

Camera and photos: the app asks for camera or photo library access only to let you add pin photos. Photos you pick or take become part of your collection. They are not sent anywhere else.

What we don't do: 

We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

How we use it:
To run your account, sync and show your collection, operate the Feed, keep it safe (moderation, abuse prevention, rate limits), and answer your feedback.

Who processes it:
Our backend (database, authentication and file storage) is hosted by Supabase. Emails such as password resets and feedback delivery are sent through Resend. These providers process data only to provide those services for us.

Moderation Feed content is subject to our community guidelines. Users can report posts and comments and block other users. Reports are reviewed by the developer, who may remove content or accounts that break the rules.

Retention and deletion We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, collection, photos, posts, comments and likes from our servers. Feedback messages and backups may be removed within [30] days. You can also email us to request deletion.

Your rights:
You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing feedback@cloison.art. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have additional legal rights, and we will honor them.

Children:

Cloison is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules on our servers. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes:

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact: feedback@cloison.art
