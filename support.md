# Cloison Support

Need help? Email feedback@cloison.art. We usually reply within [2 business days]. You can also use Settings → Send Feedback in the app.

## FAQ

### How do I add a pin? 
Tap the Add button, photograph your pin on a plain background, review the outline, then fill in the details.

### My scan outline looks wrong.
Use a plain, evenly lit background and keep the whole pin in frame. You can also adjust the outline by hand.

### How do I share a pin to the Feed?
Open the pin and choose Share to Feed. Read the community guidelines first.

### How do I report or block someone?
Tap the menu on any post or comment and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed by the developer.

### I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the email.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your data.

### Is my collection backed up?
Yes. It syncs to your account, so you can sign in on a new device to restore it.
